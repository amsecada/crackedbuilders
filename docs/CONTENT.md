# Content Guide

## Voice

Adam's voice should feel:

- intelligent
- direct
- conversational
- confident
- curious
- playful
- slightly irreverent

Avoid sounding:

- corporate
- self-important
- inspirational
- overly polished
- like a résumé
- like AI-generated marketing copy

Short sentences are welcome.

Strong statements are welcome.

Humor should feel natural rather than inserted.

---

## Writing Principle

Prefer:

> I build weird things with AI.

over:

> I am a technology entrepreneur passionate about leveraging artificial intelligence to create innovative solutions.

Prefer concrete language.

Avoid jargon unless it carries useful meaning.

---

## Hero

The hero should contain:

- Adam Secada
- short positioning statement
- primary CTA

Initial conceptual copy:

### Name

ADAM SECADA

### Eyebrow / role

BUILDER + OPERATOR

### Positioning

I build interesting things at the intersection of AI, technology, and business.

### CTA

Building something interesting?

Ping me.

These lines are provisional and should be refined once they can be evaluated inside the actual design.

---

## About

About copy should be concise.

Topics worth communicating:

- Adam is a builder and entrepreneur
- he has experience operating and scaling technology
- he is currently focused heavily on AI
- he enjoys experimenting with systems, businesses, and unusual ideas
- he prefers building things to talking abstractly about them

Avoid reproducing an entire career history.

---

## Work

Projects should communicate:

- what it is
- why it exists
- what is interesting about it
- status
- link when available

Avoid inflated startup language.

Projects may be described as experiments when they are experiments.

Not everything needs to sound like a company.

---

## Project Status Language

Preferred examples:

BUILDING
EXPERIMENT
LIVE
ARCHIVED
RESEARCH
PROTOTYPE

These statuses may also support the visual metadata system.

---

## Writing

Writing entries should emphasize the idea rather than elaborate publication metadata.

Possible subjects include:

- AI
- agent architecture
- entrepreneurship
- systems
- technology
- small business
- markets
- experiments
- things Adam is learning

Writing may initially link to external platforms.

---

## Contact

Primary concept:

Building something interesting?

Ping me.

Contact should feel inviting without becoming sales copy.

Potential secondary message:

Interesting project, weird idea, or something I should know about?

Say hello.

---

## Social Links

Likely channels may include:

- X / Twitter
- GitHub
- LinkedIn
- email

Only include platforms that are actively useful.

Do not add social links merely because they exist.

---

## Placeholder Policy

Real content should be used immediately for:

- hero
- about
- contact

Placeholders are acceptable initially for:

- project screenshots
- some project descriptions
- writing entries

Clearly mark placeholders in source code.

Do not publish fake accomplishments or invented project details.

---

## Content Density

The site should not contain large walls of text.

Prefer:

- short paragraphs
- strong headings
- concise project descriptions
- visual hierarchy

Visitors should be able to scan the entire page quickly.

---

## Final Content Test

Before publishing copy, ask:

> Would Adam actually say this out loud?

If the answer is no, rewrite it.
